    Privacy and demo safety

    Use fictional details only. Do not enter real medical information. Prescription files are never uploaded or saved; they are used only to unlock this demo checkout and are cleared when the order is placed or the page is reloaded.

    The cart, local order history and optional refill reminder dates are stored in this browser until you clear browser data. Delivery details are used only during checkout and are not retained. Firebase, when configured, stores a customer UID, order status and the latest courier location until an administrator deletes the demo record; this prototype does not automatically expire Firebase data.

    This prototype does not verify prescriptions or provide healthcare. A real launch requires a licensed pharmacy partner, pharmacist-led prescription verification and legal review.
